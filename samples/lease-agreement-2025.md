# Residential Lease Agreement — 14 Alder Court, Flat 3

*Fictional sample document for Morrow. All people, addresses and amounts are invented.*

## Parties

This lease is made on 1 March 2025 between **Halden Property Partners** (the Landlord) and **Mira Okonkwo-Reyes** (the Tenant) for the property at 14 Alder Court, Flat 3, Westmere.

## Term

The lease runs for twelve months, from 1 April 2025 to 31 March 2026. After that date it continues month to month unless either party ends it.

## Rent and deposit

The monthly rent is 1,480 dollars, due on the first day of each month by bank transfer. Rent received after the fifth day of the month incurs a late fee of 45 dollars.

The security deposit is 2,200 dollars. The Landlord must return the deposit within 21 days of the end of the tenancy, less any documented deductions for damage beyond normal wear.

## Ending the lease

The Tenant may end the lease early by giving 60 days written notice and paying an early termination charge equal to one month of rent. The Landlord must give 90 days written notice to end a month-to-month tenancy.

## Repairs and maintenance

The Landlord is responsible for the boiler, plumbing, electrical wiring and the building exterior. The Tenant must report leaks within 48 hours of noticing them. Emergency repairs can be requested at any hour through the building line, 555-0142.

The Tenant is responsible for replacing light bulbs and smoke alarm batteries, and for keeping drains clear.

## Pets and subletting

One cat or one dog under 15 kilograms is permitted with a refundable pet deposit of 300 dollars. Subletting is not allowed without the Landlord's written consent.

## Renewal

The Landlord will offer renewal terms no later than 60 days before the lease ends. Any rent increase at renewal is capped at 4 percent.
