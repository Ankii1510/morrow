# Renters Insurance Policy Summary — Policy RN-4471902

*Fictional sample document for Morrow. The insurer, policy and amounts are invented.*

## Policyholder and property

Policyholder: Mira Okonkwo-Reyes. Insured address: 14 Alder Court, Flat 3, Westmere. Insurer: Northlight Mutual.

## Policy period

Coverage runs from 1 April 2025 to 1 April 2026 and renews automatically unless cancelled 30 days before the renewal date.

## Coverage

Personal property is covered up to 30,000 dollars. Personal liability is covered up to 100,000 dollars. Additional living expenses are covered up to 6,000 dollars if the flat becomes uninhabitable after a covered loss.

Water damage from burst pipes is covered. Flood damage from rising external water is not covered. Theft is covered when there are signs of forced entry.

## Deductible and premium

The deductible is 500 dollars per claim. The annual premium is 216 dollars, billed as 18 dollars per month.

## Valuables

Single items worth more than 1,500 dollars, such as jewellery, cameras and bicycles, must be listed separately to be covered at full value. The policyholder has listed one item: a road bicycle valued at 2,100 dollars.

## Making a claim

Report a loss within 14 days through the Northlight claims line, 555-0177, or the online portal. Keep receipts and photographs of damaged items. Claims are usually decided within 10 business days.

## Landlord requirements

The lease for 14 Alder Court requires tenants to hold at least 100,000 dollars of personal liability cover. This policy meets that requirement.
